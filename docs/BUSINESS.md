# Business and Use-Case Context

## Current capability

The current implementation provides a governance layer for controlled autonomous execution, explicit policy checks, capability boundaries, and reproducible provenance. It is suitable for controlled demonstrations and engineering validation rather than broad autonomous deployment.

## Potential commercial application

Possible application domains include:

- autonomous software engineering support under policy control
- scientific research workflows that require evidence and traceability
- cybersecurity automation with explicit approval boundaries
- infrastructure operations that require safe automation and rollback
- regulated AI workflows with auditability
- high-risk agentic systems with deterministic controls

## Long-term research vision

The longer-term research agenda is to improve multi-agent coordination, stronger evidence tracking, and more formal controls over execution boundaries without overstating guarantees.
